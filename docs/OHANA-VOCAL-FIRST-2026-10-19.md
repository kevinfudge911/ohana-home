# Vocal-first music addition — October 19

Adds eleven distinct recordings from The By/For Project's *Sweet Sacrifice* album, approximately 42 minutes, to Sunday Best and a specific Vocal Christian Worship collection. Each recording's first-party page names its vocal performers and links the MP3 beside a CC BY 3.0 United States notice. The source pages' published lyrics and metadata were reviewed; solemn Passion imagery, wounds and freedom/slavery metaphors occur. These recordings are not labeled as children's songs or placed under Contemporary Christian merely because they have Christian lyrics.

The original album art comes from the project's album page. Its credits name Marcus Melton (design), Makoto Fujimura (*Golden Countenance*) and Ryann Cooley (macro photography); the first-party underlying-artwork page supplies the corresponding CC BY 3.0 United States notice. Album art is displayed proportionally without cropping. Every new recording offers a Recording & artwork credits command with text attribution and source/license links. Existing credited recordings also use their own license metadata rather than a hardcoded CC BY 4.0 label.

No family records or saved preferences are migrated. The eleven recordings work in their native collections and Play All queues. Adding these new identifiers to saved custom playlists remains disabled because the existing Worker validates a separate built-in ID allowlist; this release deliberately contains no Worker or configuration change. Existing custom playlist selections remain intact.

Two additional researched candidates remain outside this release: the optional hymn adaptation *Leaning on the Everlasting Arms*, and Pilato's *Praise You All the Time* (separate recording-rights evidence remains incomplete). Neither is represented as newly imported.

Validation: `node tests/music-playlist.cjs` exercises actual queue/transport code with isolated fixture data, including cancellation, rapid repeated taps, playlist independence, restoration, wrap and bounded failure handling. `node tests/music-vocals.cjs` checks all eleven shipped records and exercises the actual credit renderer, including its exact license, text safety and source links. All 29 inline scripts parse. In a separate isolated localhost browser harness, all eleven real source MP3s reached native Chrome readyState 4, advanced beyond 0.2 seconds, reported their expected duration and showed no media error. The original cover loaded at 501 pixels wide. These were muted initial-stream checks, not full-recording playback, audible listening, or the served Ohana application flow. Served application verification is recorded separately after deployment.

Audible listening/content audition, physical iPhone/iPad/Safari, Bluetooth and lock-screen behavior remain unverified. First-party performer credits establish the metadata basis for the vocal classification; they are not a forensic claim about audio generation.
