Recommended ink specification:
- Near-black traditional readable serif, normal case. Book/chapter running header; modest chapter initial when authentic context exists; small inline verse numerals. No oversized quote styling, centered slogan or letter spacing.
- Typeset independently on each leaf. Equal outer margins; generous inner gutter; follow perspective/curve separately. Clip ink within paper. Baseline, size and taper must transform with the leaf, not float horizontally over it.
- Paragraph prose, poetry line breaks, only accurate references. Avoid full justification on short lines; no huge word gaps. Keep a complete short verse together rather than split across leaves.
- A single-column paragraph setting per leaf is more suitable for mobile than four microscopic columns. Preserve visible art; expand actual reading view when needed. Full Bible stays above workstation; rotate portrait/landscape and check longest passage.
- Existing11 verified short NKJV verses remain exact. They cannot honestly fill continuous Bible pages. Do not fabricate context, chapter numbers, repeated text, dummy glyphs, filler or alternate translation.
