Connect Life360

  1. On a computer, open life360.com and sign in.
  2. Press F12, open Application → Cookies → life360.com.
  3. Copy the value of LIFE360_AUTH_TOKEN and paste it here.

It stays private to your Ohana Home. Life360 keeps it good for a long time; if people stop moving, paste a fresh one.
