Connect your Life360

Your Life360 shows your people, and only you see them here. Each person connects their own.
  1. On a computer, sign in at life360.com.
  2. Press F12, open Application, then Cookies, then life360.com.
  3. Copy the value named access_token and paste it here.
You see family who turn Share on, plus your own Life360 people. Nobody else sees your Life360.
