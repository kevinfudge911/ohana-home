# Ohana Town (part of Ohana Home)

town/town.json + town/*.webp = the picture and tap spots for his town screen.
Heartville Next on his phone reads these from https://ohanahome.app/town/ and from this branch,
and uses whichever has the higher "version". Change the picture or a spot here, bump "version",
and his phone shows it next time the town opens. No app install.

The public picture keeps the family photo in the heart blacked out; the phone puts his own copy back.
Same files belong in ohana-home static-assets/town/ when the worker is deployed.
