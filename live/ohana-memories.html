Share straight from your phone

On Android, install Ohana Home from Chrome’s menu, then open your Gallery or Photos, select pictures, tap Share, and choose Ohana. You’ll choose the family album before adding them.

You can also open this Memories page directly in a browser and tap Add photos. Share album sends a link; only members of that family room can view its photos.

To send an individual picture to someone outside Ohana, open it and tap Share photo. If your browser can’t share a file, Save photo lets you use your phone’s regular sharing tools.
